The philosophy

THE PHILOSOPHY

Most FP&A decks explain what happened. Finance leaders need someone who can also say why volume moved, what it means for staffing next quarter, and which lever to pull. I take messy operational data, put it in a controlled model, and hand executives a clean story with numbers they can defend.
